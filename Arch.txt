NOTE:

Limiti:
	* max 64 ground texture
	* massimo 3 incrementi di optimization level	es: con u_optlvl_increment=2 optimization level può valere 0 (num_optlvl_inc=0), 2 (num_optlvl_inc=1), 4 (num_optlvl_inc=2), 6 (num_optlvl_inc=3)
	* max 625 quadranti (1 + 12 *2)

Architettura shader

* u_grid_pos: vec2 che indica la posizione in world coordinate del quadrante in alto a sinistra dell'intera grid ovvero del vertice con x e z minori

* u_optlvl_increment: indica lo step con cui può variare l'optimization level (l'optimization level del quadrante si calcola come num_optlvl_inc * u_optlvl_increment)

* u_grid_step_optlvl_zero: distanza fra vertici contigui alla massima risoluzione in un quadrante il reale step è dato da u_grid_step_optlvl_zero * pow(2.0, float(current_optimization_level))

* u_num_quadrants_per_size: num quadrant per size in the heightmap grid

* u_grid_control_1 ==> texture FORMAT_RF, un texel per ogni quadrante (row * column) con dim = u_num_quadrants_per_size * u_num_quadrants_per_size				es with 625 per u_num_quadrants_per_size: 625 * 625 * 4 = 1.562.500 bytes per quadrant ==> 1,5MB
	* num_optlvl_inc				2 bit	range 0/3
	* low-level texture	index		6 bit	range 0/63	(per un massimo di 64 ground texture)
	* high-level texture index		6 bit	range 0/63	(per un massimo di 64 ground texture)
	* dirt texture index			6 bit	range 0/63	(per un massimo di 64 ground texture)
	* rock texture index			6 bit	range 0/63	(per un massimo di 64 ground texture)
	* reserved						6 bit

* u_grid_control_2 ==> texture FORMAT_RGBAF, un texel per ogni quadrante (row * column) con dim = u_num_quadrants_per_size * u_num_quadrants_per_size			es with 625 per u_num_quadrants_per_size: 625 * 625 * 128 = 50.000.000 bytes per quadrant ==> 48MB
	* high_elevation_factor	(R)		32 bits
	* low_elevation_factor (G)		32 bits
	* min_heigth (B)				32 bits
	* max_heigth (A)				32 bits

* u_grid_control_3 ==> texture FORMAT_RGBAF, un texel per ogni quadrante (row * column) con dim = u_num_quadrants_per_size * u_num_quadrants_per_size			es with 625 per u_num_quadrants_per_size: 625 * 625 * 128 = 50.000.000 bytes per quadrant ==> 48MB
	* slope_vertical_factor (R)		32 bits
	* slope_flat_factor (G)			32 bits
	* dirt_on_rocks_factor (B)		32 bits
	* reserved (A)					22 bits
	* heightmap_index (A)			10 bits		range 0/1023
	
* u_heightmap_array_optlvl_inc_0 to 3 ==> sampler2DArray FORMAT_RH, flat texture array (una texture per ogni quadrante pointed by the heightmap_index) con dim = u_num_quadrants_per_size * u_num_quadrants_per_size
	* heightmap ==> texture FORMAT_RH, un texel per ogni vertice (row * column) con dim = num_vertices_per_quadrant_size * num_vertices_per_quadrant_size			es with step 2 and 2049 num_vertices_per_quadrant_size: 2049 * 2049 * 2 = 8.396.802 bytes per quadrant ==> 8MB per quadrant at max resolution
		* altitude					16 bit	range -65504/+65504
		
* u_vertex_control_array_optlvl_inc_0 to 3 ==> sampler2DArray FORMAT_RF, flat texture array (una texture per ogni quadrante pointed by the heightmap_index) con dim = u_num_quadrants_per_size * u_num_quadrants_per_size
	* vertex_control ==> texture FORMAT_RF, un texel per ogni vertice (row * column) con dim = num_vertices_per_quadrant_size * num_vertices_per_quadrant_size		es with step 2 and 2049 num_vertices_per_quadrant_size: 2049 * 2049 * 4 = 16.793.604 bytes ==> 16MB per quadrant at max resolution
		* hole						1 bit	range 0/1	false/true
		* navigation				1 bit	range 0/1	false/true
		* custom texture 1 valid	1 bit	range 0/1	false/true
		* custom texture 2 valid	1 bit	range 0/1	false/true
		* custom texture 3 valid	1 bit	range 0/1	false/true
		* custom texture 4 valid	1 bit	range 0/1	false/true
		* custom texture 1 index	6 bit	range 0/63	(per un massimo di 64 ground texture)
		* custom texture 2 index	6 bit	range 0/63	(per un massimo di 64 ground texture)
		* custom texture 3 index	6 bit	range 0/63	(per un massimo di 64 ground texture)
		* custom texture 4 index	6 bit	range 0/63	(per un massimo di 64 ground texture)
		* reserved					2 bit

Es:
	* u_num_quadrants_per_size = 625
	* num_vertices_per_quadrant_size = 2049
